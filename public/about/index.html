About Nanum Learn

Nanum Learn is an educational initiative that aims to accelerate open learning by providing original and accessible resources such as books, handouts, and free classes (nanums). We aim to make the contents and resources more accessible by distributing the works under CC BY 4.0. Under the permissive license, the users are allowed to share and adapt for any purpose as long as an appropriate credit is given. For more information the use, please visit LICENSE.

What We Do

We can summarize our works and contributions into three main categories:

  1. Learn & Imagine
  2. Create Resources (e.g. Handouts, Books)
  3. Nanum (Share)

As ardent students, we first learn and imagine. We learn from numerous sources like school classes and independent study. Nanumers always begin by being curious, learning, and imagining.

As we continue to learn, we transform our knowledge and skills to something tangible like handouts and books. We create different resources with our experience of learning the materials and share them under permissive license to potentially assist intellectually curious learners out there.

One important consideration for our works is that the resources that we create are not meant to replace textbooks nor dedicated resources. Rather, they are to assist global learners who may be facing the same challenges that we faced by sharing our understanding of difficult topics in nanumer's perspectives.

Nanum Learn provides numerous open resources and opportunities such as free books, handouts, and past/current classes. We create different open resources for numerous purposes including, but not limited to individual study and teaching. Moreover through open and live classes, we wish to return the knowledge and skills we received to the world just as we learned from public resources.

Motivation

Nanum Learn started with a spark and hope to show personal appreciations. When I first started studying for math olympiads, I was surprised to know that there aren't lots of free resources for the high division of national math olympiad. Open resources were either for middle school round or preview for paid resources with high prices. Therefore, I decided to study problems from different countries. There, I was blessed by free handouts that were just there in the internet! The handouts that could have been hidden behind high paywall were freely shared for countless students just like me.

Maybe the experience is personal. However, I believe there are thousands and millions of others who experienced similar situations. This spark for gratitude let me start creating open resources that were not yet available, or yet to be more polished. Again, I wish to share my gratitude towards all contributors to open knowledge, and sincerely wishes that Nanum Learn can be a platform for open resources that inspires global learners to create and share knowledge for future learners.

— Enoch
